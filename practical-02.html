Practical 02 of 12 · Academic integrity

Assistant or ghostwriter?

The same chatbot can either help someone learn—or do the assessed work for them.

◷ 6 minutesBeginner6 step checklistFree tools only
Session tips ↗

01 / Learning objective

Compare completing an assignment with scaffolding a student’s own thinking.

02 / Follow the procedure

Check each step as you teach.

03 / Exact prompts

Click Copy exact prompt, open the linked free chat tool, paste and run. Each response can vary.

✦ A · Generate a whole draft
Write a complete 450-word undergraduate assignment titled “The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Higher Education”, with an introduction, three body paragraphs and a conclusion. Present it as a finished draft. Do not fabricate citations.
✦ B · Tutor instead of author
I need to write MY OWN 450-word university assignment on “The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Higher Education”. Do NOT write the assignment for me. Explain the key ideas, suggest an outline, ask five probing questions, recommend types of evidence to seek, and give me a checklist for evaluating my own draft.

04 / Observe & discuss

WHAT YOU MIGHT SEE

Prompt A usually produces ready-to-edit prose; Prompt B should emphasize understanding and independent work.

ASK THE AUDIENCE

What would count as an honest disclosure of AI assistance for this class?

ETHICAL / TECHNICAL SAFEGUARD

AI-assisted drafting is not universally forbidden or universally allowed. Course or institutional policy decides permitted use.

05 / Instructor answer & takeaway

Keep the answer hidden while participants predict or compare results.

The difference is not merely how polished the result looks: it is authorship, disclosure, assessment rules and whether the student actually performed the required work.
Learning checkpoint: I can explain when AI assistance becomes misrepresented authorship.
