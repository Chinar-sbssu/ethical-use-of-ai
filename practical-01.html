Practical 01 of 12 · Research integrity

The citation that never existed

A bibliography can look convincing—even when a reference is invented.

◷ 7 minutesBeginner6 step checklistFree tools only
Session tips ↗

01 / Learning objective

Distinguish AI-suggested references from verified publications; demonstrate a guaranteed fabricated control citation.

02 / Follow the procedure

Check each step as you teach.

Prepared classroom example

Use this fixed sample when consistency matters.

FICTIONAL CONTROL — DO NOT CITE AS A REAL PAPER
Author: A. Example & B. Demo (2026)
Title: Quantum Homework Telepathy Improves Every Student by 500%
Journal: Journal of Impossible Classroom Trials
DOI-like string: 10.0000/fictional-classroom-demo-2026
This is an intentionally fabricated classroom example.

03 / Exact prompts

Click Copy exact prompt, open the linked free chat tool, paste and run. Each response can vary.

✦ A · Generate references
I am preparing a literature review about generative AI and university students’ critical thinking. Suggest SIX relevant peer-reviewed papers, preferably from 2020–2026. For each, give the exact title, authors, year, venue and DOI if known. Clearly label any details you cannot verify. Do not invent a source. I will independently check each reference.
✦ B · Ask for an evidence audit
Audit your previous bibliography. For each reference, state what you actually verified with an external source, what you inferred from memory, and what remains uncertain. Do not claim that you opened Crossref or Google Scholar unless you actually did so.

04 / Observe & discuss

WHAT YOU MIGHT SEE

A candidate reference may be correct, partially wrong or unverified. The fictional control is explicitly fake and produces a repeatable teaching point.

ASK THE AUDIENCE

What is the minimum information needed to consider a citation usable in your assignment?

ETHICAL / TECHNICAL SAFEGUARD

Do not accuse a real researcher or journal of fabrication just because a search produces no result; try title variants, publisher pages and other databases.

05 / Instructor answer & takeaway

Keep the answer hidden while participants predict or compare results.

The control reference is intentionally fictional. Its citation-like format and DOI-like text are not evidence. Real AI suggestions may all be valid; independent verification is the skill being tested.
Learning checkpoint: I independently checked at least one title, author/year pair and DOI.
