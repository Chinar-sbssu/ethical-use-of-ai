Practical 12 of 12 · Decision-making

The ethics decision room

Not every AI use is obviously ethical—or obviously unethical.

◷ 7 minutesBeginner6 step checklistFree tools only
Session tips ↗

01 / Learning objective

Apply rules about authorship, privacy, disclosure and appropriate use to contextual cases.

02 / Follow the procedure

Check each step as you teach.

03 / Exact prompts

Click Copy exact prompt, open the linked free chat tool, paste and run. Each response can vary.

✦ Optional · Generate extra discussion scenarios
Create four short university classroom scenarios about the ethical use of AI. Cover academic integrity, data privacy, algorithmic bias and research citation. For each, propose the best answer: Ethical, Unethical or Depends on policy/context. Explain the reasoning in two sentences. Make every scenario fictional.

04 / Observe & discuss

WHAT YOU MIGHT SEE

Different answers surface assumptions about permissions, privacy and institutional rules.

ASK THE AUDIENCE

Which ethical AI commitment will you apply to your next research assignment?

ETHICAL / TECHNICAL SAFEGUARD

A classroom quiz does not replace the university’s actual policies; make room for justified disagreement.

05 / Instructor answer & takeaway

Keep the answer hidden while participants predict or compare results.

A policy-dependent answer can be the most responsible answer. For example, grammar assistance may be acceptable in one course and require disclosure or be prohibited in another.
Learning checkpoint: I can justify an ethical choice using transparency, consent, fairness and policy.
