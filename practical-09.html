Practical 09 of 12 · Misinformation

Make a claim, verify a claim

A realistic news format is not the same thing as evidence.

◷ 7 minutesBeginner6 step checklistFree tools only
Session tips ↗

01 / Learning objective

Practice identifying the elements of a credible story and checking if its central claim is documented.

02 / Follow the procedure

Check each step as you teach.

03 / Exact prompts

Click Copy exact prompt, open the linked free chat tool, paste and run. Each response can vary.

✦ A · Safe synthetic news demo
Write a clearly FICTIONAL 200-word news article for an academic media-literacy class. Invent a university named Northbridge Institute of Technology and a claim that it has introduced AI robot teachers. Use a plausible headline, date and quotes from invented officials, but label the TOP and BOTTOM “FICTIONAL CLASSROOM EXAMPLE”. Do not use real people, institutions or source links.
✦ B · Verification checklist
Create a concise 5-step fact-checking checklist for the following claim: “A university has replaced lecturers with robot teachers.” Include checking the original announcement, independent reporting, date, context and whether evidence supports the exact wording. Explain why no search results is not the same as proof of falsehood.

04 / Observe & discuss

WHAT YOU MIGHT SEE

You can identify journalistic conventions without any genuine corroboration. Fact Check Explorer may not have reviewed this fictional claim.

ASK THE AUDIENCE

Which evidence would persuade you that the event actually happened?

ETHICAL / TECHNICAL SAFEGUARD

Never circulate fictional reports without their prominent labels. The fact-check tool indexes published checks, not every false claim.

05 / Instructor answer & takeaway

Keep the answer hidden while participants predict or compare results.

The story is a deliberately fictional demonstration. Its formatting provides no evidence that the claimed university or event exists.
Learning checkpoint: I sought independent primary evidence rather than trusting the story’s appearance.
